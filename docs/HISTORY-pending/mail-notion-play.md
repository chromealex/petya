- Почта, Notion and Google Play are whole apps now: each keeps its connection card in «Настройки» → «Сервисы», its client and everything it answers in the chat. Saved accounts, tokens and switches stay as they were; nothing needs reconnecting.
- «Почта» owns the Google and Microsoft accounts (Gmail, Outlook.com, Microsoft 365): the card is the same, Календарь and the Google apps still sign in through it. The tool router's «почта» and the mail rules in the chat are the app's; the line «Это не полная сводка почты и календаря…» of «Что у нас сегодня?» is its section «Почта и календарь» and is there only while «Почта» is added.
- «Notion» is an app with its own catalog entry and «С чего начать» button: the same card (browser sign-in or a token), and Notion pages for task decomposition come from it.
- «Google Play» is the Google Play Console switch: ANR in the chat («ANR за неделю», follow-ups, «Повторить запрос к Google Play») and the connection card belong to it. A dashboard that asks for ANR while Google Play is off now says «добавьте «Google Play» и подключите «Google Play Console»».
- For app authors: an app can add a tool to the semantic router (`route-tool`), keep a place in the request pipeline (`chat-route`), read data for a chat subject (`dialogue-kind`), be a documentation source of the decomposition (`doc-source`) and own the check of its service card (`connection-check`, `window.addConnectionCard`); features declare `limits` and `publicFlags` of their settings (docs/extensions.md).
