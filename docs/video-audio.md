# Video and speech

MP4, MOV and WebM are accepted without an artificial limit on size or duration. Local clips are read directly from disk; Slack/Telegram videos are streamed into a temporary file that is deleted after processing. Resolution is up to 16 megapixels. For visual analysis, up to 12 frames are extracted across the entire duration, so short events may fall between frames.

The audio track is decoded sequentially using Electron in an isolated hidden window. No more than two fragments of roughly 30 seconds of PCM each are held in memory, rather than the entire uncompressed track. Fragments overlap by 0.25 seconds to reduce losses at the boundary. Local Whisper is started automatically with the existing settings. The WAV has one channel and a 16 kHz sample rate. Completely silent fragments are not sent for recognition. Reading the next fragment overlaps with recognizing the previous one; up to two recognition requests are allowed at the same time. Frames are analyzed by a pool of two tasks, and results are assembled in timestamp order. Actual parallelism depends on the local server and hardware.

Audio processing runs at roughly playback speed plus Whisper time. It does not play the clip to the user. The «Остановить чтение видео» (Stop reading video) button closes the decoder; a Whisper request that has already started will finish or time out, and the cancelled attachment is not saved.

The transcript, with the time intervals of the fragments, is passed to the same analysis as the frames. This is speech recognition, not classification of music, effects or intonation. Recognition errors are possible. If audio fails, the frames remain available, but the answer explicitly states that the audio was not fully processed. If there is no speech, Petya does not claim that the audio track is missing.

To limit the model's context, up to 40 000 characters of the transcript are kept. If this is exceeded, the remaining speech is recognized but not included in the context, and the truncation is explicitly noted. For a detailed analysis of a very long recording, it is better to attach the needed parts separately. Attachments from selected Slack/Telegram chats use this same handler.
